# R2S Creative Construction LLP Website Layout

## 1. Main Navigation

- Home
- About Us
- Services
- Projects / Portfolio
- Clients
- Contact Us

Primary header actions:

- `Get a Quote` or `Send Enquiry`
- Phone number
- Mobile menu

## 2. Page Structure

### Home

+..
Recommended sections:

1. Hero section with company name, construction capabilities, and enquiry button
2. Short company introduction
3. Core services overview
4. Selected projects with project category and location
5. Why choose R2S
6. Client logos or client list
7. Contact call-to-action
8. Footer with offices, phone, email, and quick links

### About Us

#### Who We Are / Company Overview

- Company introduction
- Experience and capabilities
- Geographic coverage
- Safety and quality commitment
- Leadership or team information, if available

#### Vision, Mission & Values

- Vision statement
- Mission statement
- Core values

#### Why Choose Us

- Technical expertise
- Integrated project delivery
- Safety and compliance
- Quality control
- Schedule and cost management
- Client-focused execution

### Services

Use a services overview page with individual detail pages or anchored sections for:

- PMC / Project Management Consultancy
- Oil & Gas
- Turnkey Projects / Civil Construction
- Compressed Biogas (CBG) Plant
- MEP Engineering
  - Fire Protection & Detection
  - HVAC Systems
  - Plumbing & Public Health Engineering
- Electrical Engineering (pending confirmation)

Each service detail page should contain:

- Service summary
- Scope of work
- Typical deliverables
- Relevant experience or project examples
- Safety, quality, and compliance information
- Enquiry call-to-action

### Projects / Portfolio

Create a filterable portfolio overview with project cards for:

- Oil & Gas Project: HPCL Refinery, Pachpadra
- Aquarium Projects
- Musical Fountain: Jio Musical Fountain
- Interior & Retail Projects
- Resorts & Landscape Projects
- Civil / Turnkey Projects

Each project detail page should contain:

- Project title and category
- Client, location, and completion status
- Project scope
- Key work completed
- Project photographs
- Results or highlights

### Clients

- Client logo grid
- Client names and sectors
- Short trust statement
- Selected case studies or testimonials, if available

### Contact Us

#### Offices

- Head Office: Surat
- Branch Office: Mumbai

#### Enquiry Form, Phone & Email

Form fields:

- Name
- Company
- Email
- Phone
- Service of interest
- Project location
- Project details
- File attachment, optional

Also include phone, email, office addresses, map links, business hours, and a success message after submission.
