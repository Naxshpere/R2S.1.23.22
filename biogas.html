The Process

From Waste to
Clean Energy

We follow a structured approach to ensure efficient design, reliable execution and long-term performance.

01

Feedstock

Agricultural, organic, industrial or municipal waste.

02

Anaerobic Digestion

Organic matter is broken down to produce biogas.

03

Biogas Upgradation

Impurities are removed to get high-purity biomethane.

04

Compression

Biomethane is compressed for storage and transport.

05

Clean Fuel

CBG is supplied for industrial, commercial and mobility use.
