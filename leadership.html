THE STORY

A deeper purpose
behind every project.

My journey began on construction sites, understanding how ideas turn into real-world infrastructure. Over the years, I have worked across large-scale industrial, commercial and institutional projects, learning deep experience in execution, project management and cross-functional coordination.

R2S was born from a simple belief — infrastructure should create long-term value for people, communities and the environment. Today, we focus on delivering engineering-led solutions in MEP, Oil & Gas and Compressed Biogas (CBG), combining discipline, innovation and sustainability.

“Real progress happens when engineering meets purpose.”
